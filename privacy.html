TRANSPARENCY

Privacy notes

What happens to information you use in these browser tools.

Local-first by default

The current tools are designed to run in your browser. FlowState tasks and PromptLab drafts are stored in this browser's local storage. DataLens processes imported files in the browser session. RoboRoute maps are handled in the browser. The current version does not provide user accounts or cloud synchronisation.

Files and exports

Choose files only when you intend to analyse them. DataLens processes supported files locally; exports are generated on your device. Do not use a shared device for sensitive information.

Notifications and integrations

Browser reminders require your permission and may depend on the browser remaining available. Google Calendar, remote push notifications and cloud backup are not connected in this release.

Your choices

You can export your work where an export option is provided, and clear locally stored data using the relevant app controls or your browser settings. Clearing browser data may remove saved projects.
