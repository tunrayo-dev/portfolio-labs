PRODUCT DIRECTION · IN DEVELOPMENT

ReadWell

A study-planning companion designed to help students organise courses, prepare for exams and build a revision routine they can actually maintain.

EXPLORE THE CONCEPT ↓SHARE FEEDBACK ↗
THE INTENTION

Useful first.
Thoughtful always.

ReadWell is planned as more than a calendar. Students will be able to enter courses, deadlines and confidence levels, then get a realistic study plan that adapts when life interrupts. The aim is to make preparation clearer and more manageable, without guilt-driven streaks or unrealistic schedules.

THE PRODUCT SYSTEM

What it is being designed to do.

01 / SETUPBuild a course workspace

Add courses, exam dates, topics, difficulty and available study time. Importing dates and course details from documents is a potential later workflow.

02 / PLANNINGCreate an adaptive study plan

Break preparation into manageable sessions, schedule practice and revisit difficult topics using spaced review. Missed sessions can be rescheduled.

03 / PRACTICELearn through active recall

Planned flashcards, topic-based practice questions and progress feedback help learners identify what needs more attention.

04 / FOCUSMake study time intentional

Flexible focus timers, break intervals and study-hour tracking support focused sessions without turning productivity into pressure.

05 / COLLABORATIONStudy with other learners

Course groups, shared notes and optional accountability features are planned for a later release, with privacy and moderation built in.

06 / EXAM MODEFeel prepared on the day

Countdowns, revision checklists and exam-day reminders help students organise the practical details as well as their revision.

Development note
This page describes the planned product direction. The features above are not yet available as a live service; integrations, payments, messaging and data handling will be validated and built before launch.
